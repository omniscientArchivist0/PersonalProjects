# Riddle Club
> A riddle created to test all skills related to riddle solving (research, cryptograph, media editing).
### Details
- The entire resolution happened through Discord.
- All levels that start with 1 are related to codebreaking.
- All levels that start with 2 are related to research.
- Players had any amount of time to solve them.
- Riddles were solved in one week.
### What I learned?
This project improved my editing skills massively, and showed to me that cleaner designs are better than busy ones.
### What I would do differently?
Adjust the difficult of the research levels.
