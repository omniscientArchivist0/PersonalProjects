# ASBE
> RPG campaign inspired by teenager dramas and my own life as a high school student.
### Details
- Eight players, happening through Discord.
- It had around 20 sessions before it entered on hiatus.
- It didn't have a system, besides normal roleplay.
- The way it worked is that it was split through scenes, and each scene represented a small part of that "episode", like a series. So, while it had eight players, they would rarely be in the same scene at the same time.
### What I learned?
It was a self-learning experience that made me discover my true skills and my weakenesses. It had me invest a huge, huge amount of time on it, even at times where I couldn't do, which led me to have burnout on that project for a while. I was able to return properly and from that point until the current hiatus, it was a big learning experience that represents some of my best work.
### What I would do differently?
I would have done less scenes, especially during the last five sessions.
