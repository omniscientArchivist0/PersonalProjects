# Sessões do Vazio
> One of my oldest campaigns and the one that inspired me to be creative. Based on Homestuck.
### Details
- It had 12 players at its peak and a total of 40 sessions.
- I created a lot of unique mechanics and additionally had to translate Homestuck mechanics into roleplay.
- It is my only campaign until this day that had an actual ending (because of how long campaigns usually tend to be).
### What I learned?
This project made me who I am. I slowly learned image editing with CorelDraw, audio editing using Audacity, how to make a campaign, how to engage the players around and even how to handle conflicts between players.
### What I would do differently?
The main thing would be to not have 12 players. 12 players proved to be a lot. Today I would probably do between 4 to 6 players.
